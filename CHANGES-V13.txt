Codenames Bilingual V13
=======================

Changes in V13:
- Restored the spymaster unrevealed board to show classic role colours only.
- Spymaster unrevealed cards no longer use the V12 white frame style and do not show images.
- Revealed cards still show the original image with a role-coloured border.
- Operative view remains the same as V12: white frame before reveal, coloured frame after reveal.
- No overlay/tint/blend mode was reintroduced.
